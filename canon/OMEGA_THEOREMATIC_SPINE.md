## T-ΩP-01 — Host-transport non-promotion
**Status:** THEOREM_TARGET

Transport, formatting, summarization, indexing, routing, serialization or provider migration without new independently admissible evidence/witness cannot by itself strengthen epistemic or authority status.

## T-ΩP-02 — Non-escalation by orchestration
**Status:** THEOREM_TARGET

Composition of agents, tools or providers without a new lawful authority witness cannot generate an authority class stronger than the relevant execution path grants.

## T-ΩP-03 — Provenance conservation under semantic transport
**Status:** THEOREM_TARGET

A semantics-preserving transport must retain enough source identity and transformation history to reconstruct the public object's claimed lineage; information loss must be explicit.

## T-ΩP-04 — No silent identity substitution
**Status:** DERIVED_LAW / FORMALIZATION TARGET

Replacing a model or provider does not by itself replace Station identity when declared identity roots, canonical state and migration witnesses are preserved.

## T-ΩP-05 — Reinstantiation sufficiency
**Status:** THEOREM_TARGET

Define a minimal portable state whose successful replay is sufficient to reinstantiate a conformant Station projection on a new substrate.

## T-ΩP-06 — Witnessed cross-level promotion
**Status:** THEOREM_TARGET

Any transition changing epistemic status, canonical authority, execution authority, identity binding or model/world bridge must carry a typed witness naming source state, target state, transformation, residuals and obligations.

## T-ΩP-07 — Effect-receipt soundness
**Status:** THEOREM_TARGET

A command or API success cannot establish an external-world effect unless the declared effect predicate is observed through an admissible witness channel.
